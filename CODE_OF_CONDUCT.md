# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent; ask for clarification when unsure.
- Focus feedback on the work, not the person.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others’ private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues/PRs, or ban contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate. To report a concern, contact the maintainer via GitHub (Nortaq-PlayNexus).
